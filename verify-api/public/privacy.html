Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2025

Auto-Pay ("we", "our", or "us") operates the auto-payment.ximanta.xyz website and API.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we request read-only access to your Gmail inbox (gmail.readonly) and permission to configure email forwarding (gmail.settings.sharing). We use this solely to verify UPI payment notifications sent by FamApp.

2. How We Use Your Data

We do not read, store, or sell your personal emails. Our system only scans for emails matching the FamApp payment template. The payment amount and UTR are temporarily stored in Cloudflare KV for verification purposes and are deleted immediately after your API key is issued.

3. Data Retention

Your Google OAuth refresh token and API key are stored securely in Cloudflare KV. You can revoke this access at any time via our dashboard or your Google Account permissions page.

4. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us at realximanta@gmail.com.
